How break-even is calculated

Break-even is the sell price where what you receive after the sell fee equals everything you spent.

(buy cost + fees + extra costs) ÷ (coins you can sell × (1 − sell fee rate))

  • Fee in quote currency: added to your cost when buying, taken from proceeds when selling.
  • Fee in coins, buying: you receive fewer coins, so you own quantity × (1 − rate). Your cost is unchanged.
  • Fee in coins, selling: assumed to come out of the coins you sell, since you sell everything you hold. The result matches a quote-currency sell fee.
  • Fee in another token: you enter its worth in quote currency. It is counted once.
  • Finished trades: your real order amounts are used as entered. Fees marked included are never added again.
  • Gross and net: gross is the price move only. Net is after all fees and costs. ROI is net ÷ total cost.

Nothing is rounded during calculation, only for display. Estimates can differ from your exchange statement, which is the source of truth.
